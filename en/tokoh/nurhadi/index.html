Gratification and Money Laundering by the Former Supreme Court Secretary

GratuitiesMoney laundering
Appeal ruling20 May 2026

Gratification from parties to court cases in 2013–2019 and money laundering, according to the Jakarta Anti-Corruption Court, upheld by the High Court.

Role: Sentenced to 5 years; upheld by the Jakarta High Court. His lawyer said he would advise a cassation, but as of October 2026 we have found no report that one was filed or decided.

Timeline

  1. 30 June 2025Detained[4]

  4. 20 May 2026Appeal ruling[2]

Bribery and Gratification over Case Handling by the Supreme Court Secretary

BriberyGratuities
Final verdict

Bribes and gratification received together with his son-in-law Rezky Herbiono to arrange cases in the courts, according to the Jakarta Anti-Corruption Court.

Role: Sentenced to 6 years. Left Sukamiskin prison on 29 June 2025 and was immediately re-arrested in the money-laundering case.

Timeline

  1. March 2021Convicted (first instance)[3]
