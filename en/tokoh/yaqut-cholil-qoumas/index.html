Alleged Corruption in the Allocation of the Additional 2023-2024 Hajj Quota

State loss
On trial11 Aug 2026

An alleged corruption case over the allocation of the additional 2023-2024 hajj quota at the Ministry of Religious Affairs. KPK prosecutors allege, based on a BPK audit, that filling the additional special-hajj quota caused a loss to the state. The trial is ongoing and there is no verdict yet.

Role: Charged together with his former special adviser Ishfah Abidal Aziz, Ismail Adham (Maktour) and Asrul Azis Taba (Kesthuri). Prosecutors say he was enriched by USD 271,500; Yaqut denies receiving the money and calls the indictment imprecise. His pretrial motion and his objection to the indictment were both rejected, and the trial has moved on to witness hearings.
