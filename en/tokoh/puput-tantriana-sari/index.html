Gratification and Money Laundering by the Regent of Probolinggo

GratuitiesMoney laundering
Convicted (first instance)13 Feb 2025

Gratification and money laundering together with her husband Hasan Aminuddin, according to the first-instance ruling of the Surabaya Anti-Corruption Court. Prosecutors and the defendants appealed.

Cases · KPK · 2021 · Jawa Timur

Bribery for Acting Village-Head Posts in Probolinggo

Bribery
Final verdict

Bribes received over acting village-head appointments in Probolinggo in 2021, together with her husband Hasan Aminuddin, according to the Surabaya Anti-Corruption Court.

Role: Sentenced to 4 years; the Supreme Court rejected the cassation in January 2023, so the 4-year sentence stands. Reported in May 2024 to be still serving this sentence.
