Tax Official Rafael Alun Gratification and Money Laundering

GratuitiesMoney laundering
Final verdict

Gratification and money laundering by a tax official, uncovered after the KPK examined a wealth declaration that omitted several assets.

Role: Sentenced to 14 years; cassation appeals by both the defendant and the KPK were rejected, with some evidence ordered returned.

Timeline
