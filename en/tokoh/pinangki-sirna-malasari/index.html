Bribery over a Supreme Court Fatwa for Djoko Tjandra

BriberyMoney laundering
Final verdict

Money received from fugitive Djoko Tjandra over a plan to obtain a Supreme Court fatwa, plus money laundering, according to the court verdict.

Role: Sentenced to 10 years, more than the 4 years prosecutors sought. The High Court cut it to 4 years, citing among other things her admission of guilt, her dismissal and her young child. Neither side filed a cassation appeal.
