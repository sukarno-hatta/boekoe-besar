Cases · Kejaksaan · 2025 · DKI Jakarta

Bribery for the Release Verdict in the CPO Export Case

Bribery
Final verdict

Bribes to arrange release verdicts for corporate defendants in the CPO export corruption case at the Central Jakarta District Court, according to the indictment and the courts.

Role: Presiding judge in the CPO export case. Sentenced to 11 years, increased to 12 years on appeal. The Supreme Court rejected both his cassation and the prosecutors', making the ruling final.
