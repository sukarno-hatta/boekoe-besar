Cases · KPK · 2022 · Papua Tengah

Corruption in the Kingmi Mile 32 Church Construction, Mimika

State loss
Final verdict

Corruption in phase 1 of the Kingmi Mile 32 church construction in Mimika (2015 budget), with a state loss of about Rp21.6 billion according to the KPK.

Role: The Makassar Anti-Corruption Court released him, ruling his acts were not a crime; the Supreme Court granted the KPK's cassation and sentenced him to 2 years. Dismissed as regent in May 2024.
