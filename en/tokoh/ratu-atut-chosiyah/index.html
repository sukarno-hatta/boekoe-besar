Cases · KPK · 2013 · Banten

Lebak Election Dispute Bribery at the Constitutional Court

Bribery
Final verdict

Payment of Rp1 billion to then Constitutional Court Chief Justice Akil Mochtar over the 2013 Lebak regency election dispute, according to the court verdict.

Role: Found to have paid the bribe together with her brother, Tubagus Chaeri Wardana. The Supreme Court rejected her cassation appeal and increased the sentence to 7 years.

Timeline

  1. 1 September 2014Convicted (first instance)[1]
    PN Tipikor Jakarta · Prison: 4 yr

    4 years and a Rp200 million fine. Prosecutors sought 10 years and revocation of political rights, which the court did not impose.

  2. 23 February 2015Cassation ruling[2]
    Mahkamah Agung · Prison: 7 yr

    Cassation rejected; sentence increased to 7 years.

  3. 6 September 2022Released on parole[4]

    Released on parole from Tangerang prison; under supervision of the Serang probation office.

Cases · KPK · 2013 · Banten

Banten Medical Equipment Procurement Corruption

State lossExtortion
Convicted (first instance)20 Jul 2017

Rigging of the budget and tender for medical equipment at the Banten provincial referral hospital in fiscal year 2012, and extortion of agency heads, according to the court verdict.

Role: Sentenced to 5 years 6 months, added to her prison term. The court also found she extorted Rp500 million from subordinates. She had returned Rp3.8 billion. According to The Jakarta Post, she told the court she accepted the verdict; the sources we found do not say whether KPK prosecutors appealed.
