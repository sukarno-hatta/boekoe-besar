Conspiracy to Bribe in the Ronald Tannur Cassation and Gratuities over Supreme Court Cases

BriberyGratuities
Final verdict

A conspiracy to give or promise money to a Supreme Court justice in the Gregorius Ronald Tannur cassation, and the receipt of gratuities from parties to court cases, according to the courts. The case grew out of the Attorney General's Office investigation into Ronald Tannur's acquittal at the Surabaya District Court.

Role: Sentenced to 16 years, increased to 18 years on appeal. The Supreme Court rejected both his cassation and the prosecutors', making the ruling final. The Attorney General's Office said he was moved to Salemba prison to serve his sentence on 8 December 2025.
