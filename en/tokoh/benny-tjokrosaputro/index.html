Corruption in the Management of PT Asabri Funds

State loss
Appeal ruling

Corruption in the management of PT Asabri funds. The court gave Benny a "nil" sentence because he was already serving life for Jiwasraya, but ordered restitution.

Role: Prosecutors sought the death penalty; the court imposed a nil sentence plus restitution. Prosecutors and the defence appealed, and the Jakarta High Court upheld the ruling. We have not found information on a cassation.
