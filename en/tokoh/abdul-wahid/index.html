Alleged Extortion over Extra Funding for the Riau Public Works Agency

ExtortionGratuities
Appeal ruling5 Oct 2026

An extortion case over extra funding for road and bridge units of the Riau public works agency in 2025. The first-instance court convicted under the gratification article, and the Riau High Court increased the sentence; the ruling is not final.

Role: Sentenced to 2 years under the gratification article. His lawyer says the extortion charge was not proven; both he and the KPK appealed. The Riau High Court raised the sentence to 4 years because the first ruling fell below the article's minimum sentence. As of 5 October 2026 the KPK was still weighing its next step.
