Cases · KPK · 2020 · Nasional

COVID-19 Social Aid Bribery

Bribery
Final verdict1 Sept 2021

Collection of a Rp10,000 fee per COVID-19 social aid package from suppliers at the Ministry of Social Affairs, according to KPK prosecutors.

Role: Sentenced to more than prosecutors requested. Neither the defendant nor the KPK appealed, so the verdict became final.

Timeline

  1. 6 December 2020Suspect[5]
