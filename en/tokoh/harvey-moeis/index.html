Tin Commodity Trade Corruption (PT Timah)

State lossMoney laundering
Final verdict

Corruption in tin trading within PT Timah's mining concession, 2015–2022. The court found state financial and economic losses of around Rp300 trillion, including environmental damage.

Role: Sentence increased from 6.5 to 20 years on appeal; cassation rejected by the Supreme Court.
