Ministry of Trade Sugar Import Corruption

State loss
Presidential abolition (prosecution ended)1 Aug 2025

Case over raw sugar import permits at the Ministry of Trade. No restitution was ordered because the court found the defendant received none of the money.

Role: Sentenced to 4.5 years at first instance and appealed. The President granted abolition through Presidential Decree No. 18/2025, effective 1 August 2025, ending the prosecution.

Timeline

  1. 29 October 2024Suspect[3]

    Named a suspect by the Attorney General's Office.

  2. 18 July 2025Convicted (first instance)[2]
    PN Tipikor Jakarta Pusat · Prison: 4 yr 6 mo

    4 years 6 months and a Rp750 million fine; prosecutors sought 7 years. No restitution ordered.

  3. 1 August 2025Presidential abolition (prosecution ended)[4]

    Presidential Decree No. 18/2025 on abolition took effect on 1 August 2025.
