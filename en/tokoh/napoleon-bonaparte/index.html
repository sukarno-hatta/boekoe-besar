Bribery to Remove Djoko Tjandra's Red Notice

Bribery
Final verdict

Bribes from the fugitive Djoko Tjandra, routed through businessman Tommy Sumardi, to help remove his name from the immigration wanted list, according to the Jakarta Anti-Corruption Court.

Role: Sentenced to 4 years; upheld on appeal and cassation. Sent to Cipinang prison in November 2021.

Timeline

  1. 10 March 2021Convicted (first instance)[2]
    PN Tipikor Jakarta Pusat · Prison: 4 yr

    4 years and a Rp100 million fine.

  2. July 2021Appeal ruling[3]
    Pengadilan Tinggi DKI Jakarta · Prison: 4 yr

    The 4-year sentence was upheld.

  3. 3 November 2021Cassation ruling[1]
    Mahkamah Agung · Prison: 4 yr

    Cassations by the defendant and prosecutors were rejected.
