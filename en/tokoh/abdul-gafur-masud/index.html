Procurement and Licensing Bribery in Penajam Paser Utara

Bribery
Final verdict

Bribery over the arrangement of work packages and licensing in the Penajam Paser Utara regency government, uncovered in a KPK sting on 12 January 2022.

Role: Found to have received bribes totalling Rp5.7 billion over the arrangement of work packages in the regency.

Timeline

  1. 13 January 2022Suspect[8]

    Announced the day after the 12 January 2022 sting.

  2. 13 January 2022Detained[8]

    Detained at the KPK detention centre.

  3. August 2022Prosecution demand[2]
    Prison: 8 yr

    KPK prosecutors sought 8 years.

  4. September 2022Convicted (first instance)[3]
    PN Tipikor Samarinda · Prison: 5 yr 6 mo

    Case no. 33/Pid.Sus-TPK/2022/PN Smr

    5 years 6 months, Rp300 million fine or 4 months, Rp5.7 billion restitution or 3 years 6 months. Outlets differ on the hearing day: Monday 26 September (IDN Times, Hukumonline) or Tuesday 27 September 2022 (CNN Indonesia).

  5. date unknownPolitical rights revoked[1]

    Right to stand for office revoked for 3 years 6 months.

  6. date unknownFinal verdict[1]

    Verdict final; transferred to Balikpapan Class IIA prison on 19 October 2022.

  7. 31 August 2026Released on parole[9]

    Paroled from Balikpapan Class IIA prison; must report monthly, with probation expected to run until 2030. According to the prison, the fines (Rp600 million) and restitution (over Rp11 billion) from both cases have been paid.

Corruption in Capital Injections into Penajam Paser Utara Regional Enterprises

State loss
Convicted (first instance)4 Sept 2024

Corruption in 2019–2021 capital injections into the Penajam Benuo Taka and Penajam Benuo Taka Energi regional enterprises.

Role: Found guilty over capital injections made without adequate legal basis, study or administration.

Timeline

  1. date unknownProsecution demand[7]
    Prison: 7 yr

    Prosecutors sought 7 years and a Rp600 million fine.

  2. 4 September 2024Convicted (first instance)[7]
    PN Tipikor Samarinda · Prison: 6 yr

    6 years, Rp300 million fine or 3 months. The deputy head of the Samarinda court put restitution at about Rp6.2 billion (a rounded figure, not recorded as an amount), with Rp3 billion already returned during the trial. After the hearing he said he would appeal; we have not found the outcome of any appeal.
