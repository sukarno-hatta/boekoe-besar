Cases · KPK · 2021 · Sulawesi Selatan

South Sulawesi Infrastructure Project Bribery and Gratification

BriberyGratuities
Final verdict

Bribes and gratification from contractors over infrastructure projects in the South Sulawesi provincial government, according to the Makassar corruption court.

Role: Sentenced to 5 years, less than the 6 years prosecutors sought. He did not appeal, and the KPK enforced the verdict in December 2021. After a presidential decree dated 12 January 2022, the South Sulawesi council approved his removal as governor. He served his sentence at Sukamiskin prison until parole in August 2023.
