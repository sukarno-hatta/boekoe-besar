KPK · 2017 · Nasional

Electronic ID Card (e-KTP) Procurement Corruption

State loss

Corruption in the Rp5.9 trillion electronic ID card procurement project at the Ministry of Home Affairs, with estimated state losses of up to Rp2.3 trillion.

State loss
Rp 2.3 trillion[1]

Estimate, 'up to Rp2.3 trillion'

People in this case (1)

Setya Novanto

Speaker of the House of Representatives

Final verdict

Found guilty in the e-KTP corruption case. Sentence reduced on case review (PK) in 2025; released on parole in August 2025 with reporting obligations until 2029.

Prison
12 yr 6 mo
Fine imposed
Rp 500M[2]
Restitution
Rp 49.1B[2]

Timeline
