Corruption in Capital Injections into Penajam Paser Utara Regional Enterprises

State loss

Corruption in 2019–2021 capital injections into the Penajam Benuo Taka and Penajam Benuo Taka Energi regional enterprises.

Rp 14.5 billion[1]

Per the Samarinda corruption court verdict

Presumption of innocence

This person has not been found guilty by a final, binding verdict in the cases marked "in process". What follows reflects charges and proceedings, not our conclusions.

Timeline

  2. 4 September 2024Convicted (first instance)[2]
    PN Tipikor Samarinda · Prison: 6 yr
