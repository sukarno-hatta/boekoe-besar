BAKTI Kominfo 4G Base Tower (BTS) Corruption

State loss

Corruption in the procurement of 4G base towers and supporting infrastructure by BAKTI Kominfo. The state auditor (BPKP) calculated state losses at Rp8.32 trillion.

State loss
Rp 8.3 trillion[1]

BPKP calculation

People in this case (1)

Johnny Gerard Plate

Minister of Communication and Information Technology

Final verdict

Sentenced to 15 years; cassation and case review were both rejected by the Supreme Court.

Prison
15 yr
Fine imposed
Rp 1B[2]
Restitution

Timeline
